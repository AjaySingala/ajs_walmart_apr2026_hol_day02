LLMs work on Probability:
- Which is the next best word to be returned to the user?

Tokens: words broken into tokens.
Numbers: are numerical representation of the tokens.
	- Dictionary (of words)
	- LLMs have a dictionary (vocabulary) of tokens (numerical) in different supported languages.
	- it is not just words in the vocabulary. It is also about punctuations, special chars, etc.
	- One word is broken into multiple tokens.

Embeddings:
Giving meaning to the tokens given a context.

Blood Bank		- Medicinal
Bank of a River	- water-bodies
ICICI Bank		- Money

Embs are numerical representation of the "location" of the word/token in a gi en multi-dimension vector.

They are like GPS co-ords.
bank: [0.24, 0.56, 0.87]

loan from a bank : bank - [0.24, 0.56, 0.87]
river bank: bank - [0.40, 0.34, 0.98]
blood bank: bank - [0.20, 0.30, 0.50]

Search using Embeddings:
- do not search word-to-word.
- not a literal word search.
- It is a "similarity" or "semantic" search.
